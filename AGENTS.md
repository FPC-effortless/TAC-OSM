## TAC-OSM capability priority — owner direction 2026-10-23

**Develop real learned abilities first; attribution is optional diagnosis, not the goal.** For the next TAC model iteration, prefer one **integrated native world model** that is actually trained, independently replayed and evaluated on authentic observed outcomes for *new useful capabilities*: multi-horizon prediction, uncertain timing, carried and corrected entity state, missingness resilience, and later action/outcome consequences if factual intervention records exist. Use proven mathematical techniques and existing research rather than many tiny diagnostic experiments. Prioritize genuine end-to-end ability over proving which memory cell caused it, CPU micro-optimization, or a new GRU comparison every iteration.

A single-architecture `capability_development` preregistration **may train and report its own authentic developmental scores** without a matched ablation/comparator matrix, but **may not claim architecture superiority or memory-module attribution**. Only require equal parameter/data/exposure/compute and matched competitors when asserting a comparative winner. The prior 64-event kill rule concerns a narrow TAC-memory-*superiority* claim, not permission to abandon full-model capability development. Never call an implementation unit test a learned skill, use synthetics to claim real-world success, open a sealed TEST for tuning, or skip in-repository original checkpoints and independent clean-clone replay. See `research/MASTER_RESEARCH_PLAN.md` owner-priority section.

## Investor-readiness research steering — owner direction 2026-10-23

**Two linked north stars:** (1) improve the **actually trained, complete TAC-OSM** model's useful world-state/forecasting/uncertainty/action-outcome abilities, and (2) translate at least one credible ability into **independently measured customer value** and eventually investment readiness. These are **not equivalent**: research model results ≠ verified production deployments ≠ actual customer ROI ≠ willingness to pay. Read the binding [research master plan's investor/capability section](research/MASTER_RESEARCH_PLAN.md) before selecting the next substantial research or product-integration milestone.

**Default work priority:** an integrated real-data-trained model with meaningful, task-matched predictive gains; then a minimal **permission-bound advisory** workflow API or dashboard and independently consented customer pilot; then evidence of business value. Begin with a single enterprise workflow delay/failure-risk use case unless real customer discovery shows another stronger wedge. Cross-domain/physical research remains valid but separate its evidence from the initial commercial case. Do not optimize novelty, CPU microbenchmarks, branding or ablations at the expense of true useful model skills.

**For every new capability-development registration**, append a concise `Capability-to-Value Card` stating the target model skill, original lawful observed data/time-cut, evaluated forecast denominator and proper scoring, practical operator decision it could support, external-pilot evidence status, original Git checkpoint/replay and the business stage actually unlocked. Research-only work is allowed: mark downstream customer relevance `UNKNOWN/NOT_MEASURED`, not fabricated. Never present a DEV-only result or simulated integration as a validated paying customer or future monetary benefit.

**Promotion gates:** exploratory research-oriented pre-seed conversations may start now but funding is not guaranteed. Commercial seed / enterprise-readiness claims require independently authorized actual pilots, appropriate customer forecasts and costs, demonstrably safe product integration, buyer/revenue evidence and legal/privacy/IP review. Do not report a specific company, contract, license or deployed integration as established without verifying it. Parameter/data/compute parity remains compulsory **for architecture superiority claims**, not for single-architecture capability training. Original real-world evaluation, previously exposed split disclosure, exact Git-owned model bytes and independently replayed scores remain compulsory. Never automatically merge, deploy, grant policy authority or make external customer contact.

## Investor-readiness as a parallel research objective — owner direction 2026-10-23

**Capability development stays first, but it must increasingly demonstrate credible real-world customer usefulness.** Read `research/INVESTOR_READINESS_AND_CAPABILITY_ROADMAP.md` alongside `research/MASTER_RESEARCH_PLAN.md` when selecting any new native training study, capability claim, advisory workflow interface or commercialization deliverable. The initial **unvalidated** product hypothesis is *read-only enterprise workflow forecasting* (what changes next, when, how uncertain, what state is now, and whether warnings arrive early enough to help a human). Robotics/physical world research remains part of the technical program; do not assert that one domain generalizes to another.

Before each new study or capability milestone: (1) name the whole-model ability, authentic source and prediction-time data contract; (2) name an operator/decision question it could ultimately improve **without inventing customers**; (3) train the integrated model where an actual real-data score is feasible; (4) preserve each original trained checkpoint and score replay in Git; (5) report both **capability evidence** and **potential operational relevance**, separating confirmed facts from product/customer hypotheses. Prefer multi-horizon forecasting, coherent uncertainty, verified state corrections and source-supported long histories over further tiny ablations or CPU micro-optimizations. Use strong practical references and matched rivals only where necessary to quantify utility or back architectural-superiority claims, not as blockers for single-architecture development.

**Investor stages are independent facts:** research pre-seed discussions may be considered based on an honest prototype and team; **do not claim** enterprise seed/growth readiness, independent pilots, revenue, third-party ROI, production deployments, exclusive IP, company formation, GDPR/SOC2 compliance, or fundraising without documentary evidence. Targets of 2–3 design partners and >=1 genuine external pilot are *aspirational*, not completed. Do not autonomously solicit investors, sign contracts, access restricted customer data, establish entities, change license, patent, deploy or merge absent specific authority. Record model ability, independent proof, product readiness, partner validation, IP/legal/security and commercial evidence separately. New original source access and no-test-leakage controls remain compulsory.

## Mandatory TAC-OSM real-research gateway (new model work)

For any proposed TAC-OSM model-training or architecture-comparison experiment,
read `research/REAL-WORLD-EVIDENCE-RULES.md` and
`research/CAPABILITY-FIRST-RESEARCH-RESET-2026-10-22.md` first. Do not begin
new ME/DEV training when the machine-checkable preregistered
`research/mathematical-engineering/<study>/FAIR_COMPARISON.json` is absent
or fails `scripts/validate_research_parity.py --phase preflight`. Require
original matched trainable parameters, real data/order, training and tuning
budgets, and identical evaluation conditions before any architecture winner
is discussed; report CPU time as an efficiency metric, not a parity gate;
run `--phase final` on honest resource receipts after training. A text caveat
is not parity. Research novelty starts with peer-reviewed prior-art reuse and
one unresolved capability objective, not a small recurrent-memory ablation.
Legacy studies without this gate are immutable historical evidence, NOT
prospectively qualified superiority claims. Keep work in draft branches.


**Scientific-rule clarification (2026-10-22):** CPU wall-clock/process time
is an efficiency measurement and is never required to match between
architectures. The compulsory fair-comparison requirements are model
parameters, identical real training data/exposure, matched training/tuning
budgets and equal evaluation conditions; see the latest amendment to
`research/REAL-WORLD-EVIDENCE-RULES.md`. Historical CPU observations are
preserved but do not make a comparison scientifically invalid by themselves.

## Binding 100k trained-model scale (effective 2026-10-23)

For EVERY future trained TAC-OSM neural model or comparator (including event/robot models, one-off fits and each seed), require **100,000 trainable parameters ±5% inclusive (95,000–105,000)** AND the same band for all deployed learned model parameters. For matched architecture studies, independently require **≤1% relative parameter gap between every trained arm** on exactly matching real data/budget. Count ALL active encoder, fast/slow/associative memory, concept, shared-weight decoder, routing, action, forecast, uncertainty and policy heads; no dummy padding or bypass with a frozen pretrained encoder. Measure the actual instantiated model **BEFORE the first optimizer step** and remeasure the actual final model at the postfit gate. Use `scripts/validate_research_parity.py --manifest ... --phase preflight` and `--phase final`, or `--model-parameters COUNT --deployed-parameters COUNT` for a standalone prefit. For solo training use `study_type: single_model_training`; prospective diagnostics are explicitly no-fit. If outside the band or missing receipts, DO NOT TRAIN. Do not relax the matched data, training compute, checkpoints/replay or real-world evaluation obligations, and do not require equal CPU time. Historical unchanged ME001–ME072/DEV001–DEV061 experiments and their existing model bytes remain as originally measured; any NEW trained fit belongs to this prospective rule regardless of recycled old code or paths. See rule 12 in `research/REAL-WORLD-EVIDENCE-RULES.md`.

## Compulsory live enforcement (2026-10-23)

All fitting paths, including capability development and source-deferred studies,
use `src/tac_osm/parameter_budget.py`. Construct optimizers with
`budgeted_optimizer(whole_model, optimizer_type, ..., budget_manifest=...)`,
update with `checked_optimizer_step`, and persist originals with
`save_training_checkpoint`. Prefer `scripts/guarded_research.py` as the launcher.
The guard measures the actual whole model before any optimizer update, checks
both counts against the preregistered arm/seed, writes a durable receipt before
the first update, and binds saved model tensors to it. Final validation requires
that receipt and the actual checkpoint, not declared counts alone. Run
`scripts/audit_training_budget.py` when adding entrypoints; CI rejects raw
PyTorch constructors, updates and saves in supported repository training code.
A reused historical study ID never exempts a new fit. Replay-only exceptions
require an explicit original registration, full Git commit and checkpoint hashes
in `research/governance/FROZEN_REPLAY_REGISTRY.json`. They authorize integrity
replay only. Do not rewrite old registrations to manufacture pre-update evidence.
See `research/governance/LIVE_PARAMETER_ENFORCEMENT.md` for the contract and limits.

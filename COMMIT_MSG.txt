Fix delta_K to measure the K-th competitor, not the K-th overall score

delta@K was documented as `s_gold - s_Kth-competitor` in both measurement
scripts and in MEASUREMENT_LAYERS.md, but implemented as the gap to the K-th
*overall* score with gold included. Since the K-th overall score *is* gold
whenever gold sits in the top K, delta@1 was zero by construction for any
scorer that ranked gold first: the analytic vector reported +0.0000 where its
true margin is +3.0.

The key was computed in both scripts but never printed or read by anything
downstream, which is why the definition and the computation were able to
disagree for as long as they did. Audit 8 in TACOSM-MATCHED-001 records the
mechanism and the proof.

  the reproduction gate compares delta_1 only, computed from

# MODEL02-BACKBLAZE-2014-001: pre-acquisition longitudinal source study

**Scientific tier:** exact prospective research registration and unit-testable source protocol, not an actual trained, evaluated or externally deployed model. This is a distinct source/year from the now-exposed 2013 Backblaze development periods. No original 2014 ZIP bytes, original drive identities, source case labels or future TEST windows have been opened in this work.

## Source licensing, events and financial decision meaning

Official source: [Backblaze hard-drive reliability test data](https://www.backblaze.com/cloud-storage/resources/hard-drive-test-data); published [2014 annual Drive Stats ZIP](https://f001.backblazeb2.com/file/Backblaze-Hard-Drive-Data/data_2014.zip), listed by the publisher as roughly **587 MB compressed** and comprising 365 daily CSV files. Original publicly described failure=1 is the drive's last operational day before its recorded failure; disappearance without that flag can reflect repair/replacement/upgrade, not a negative or confirmed failure. Cite Backblaze and do not resell its raw dataset. The original 2014 archive is NOT downloaded here, and SHA256 cannot be invented.

Even where a publisher dates a daily snapshot, publication after the fact does not establish that external customers could have obtained that record at the original inference time. We require separate original available-at-time receipts to qualify a prospective operational claim. The data contain no original device replacement/repair interventions or real avoided decision costs; economic benefit is not measured.

## Freeze before acquiring source

`specialist_models/model02_backblaze_2014_protocol.py` defines unaltered 2014 half-open chronological cohorts: FIT January 1–June 30, CAL July 1–August 31, exposed DEV September 1–October 31, and **protected TEST November 1–December 31**. A potential risk decision requires seven consecutive original observed nonfailure days including the decision day; never enroll using whether a future failure was eventually observed. A future failure outcome requires a publisher terminal-failure flag within seven *continuously observed* next days of the **same phase**; missing followup or crossing a phase boundary is right-censored, never a negative label.

The code forbids any protected TEST scoring and refuses all qualified training authorization. The fixed research agenda requires independent per-drive enrollment evidence, right censoring and left truncation, manufacturer/model differences, per-original-day file hashes, owner data rights, substantive publisher-time source qualification and original checkpoint/optimizer/RNG Git replay. Unmatched model training isn't a permitted TAC-vs-control comparison.

## Scientific tests planned (not performed)

Start with training-source prevalence, current-day source SMART logistic, causal seven-day history summary and a proper survival/censor-aware baseline; score original probability quality and a preregistered operator ranking budget on distinct physical drives, with cluster-corrected uncertainty. Only where true source data, independent evaluation and normal research compute/data/parameter controls permit should the roughly 100k native TAC memory comparator be trained. Protect November–December source records from informal examination or tuning.

The future initial source audit will require an original publisher archive SHA256 and original daily-file digests plus independently supported source timing, identity, observable outcome flags and lifecycle. A *new* source run must have complete receipts before permissioned training. CI here uses **constructed day/flag histories only**, and is not real-world model evidence or a source acquisition success.

## Why this matters

Prior exposed 2013 source studies found that outcome flags in the entire source population cannot be treated as events among the first day's fixed 900-drive cohort. Past-only rolling admission and censoring matter, and Brier/NLL ranking can disagree with an operator's limited review capacity. These are preregistered constraints to avoid repeating those errors; they do not prove a new 2014 skill or any TAC memory advantage.

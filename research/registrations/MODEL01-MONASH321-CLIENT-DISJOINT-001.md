## Forecast target and source limitations

- No-fit baselines are 1-hour and 24-hour ahead **hourly load kW** per original client with 168 past source hourly values (at most one week). Use the most recent observed load, previous day's same clock hour, previous week's same hour, and an explicit 50/50 day/week reference. No fitting, hidden labels, smoothing using future values, seasonality chosen with DEV labels or final protected TEST access.
- Score final fixed 336 forecast anchors from each CAL and DEV customer series (up to 336 hours before source end), only if at least 24 of last 168 readings are positive; exclude 72h around each March/October Portugal DST transition in the **published local timestamp construction**. These conditions are source-time-quality *heuristics* and do not prove native observed vs padded values. Reports must include number excluded/inactive/DST per client, at both horizons.
- Evaluate genuine per-client MAE and RMSE kW for each method and horizon; macro-average MAE by physical customer and include pooled count. **No median-or-best-seed cherry-picking**, no selecting winning simple reference based on protected customer labels. For future candidate promotion, preregister ≥5% client-macro MAE gain over the **best** no-fit reference at both horizons, zero catastrophic customer regressions by registered group, nonworsened uncertainty/coverage and 95% independent customer-cluster paired intervals before first neural scoring.
- Upstream UCI **already pads zeros before some customers are active** and encodes Portuguese DST transition hours with source-specific zeros/aggregation. Monash hourly transformation further changes source format. Zero is not automatically a genuine missingness-free negative; DST hour local timestamps are not reliable UTC observation-available-at. An actual deployment would require source-owned receipt / `available_at` time; this is missing. `@missing false` is file metadata, not proof of original sensor sampling reliability. No early-real-time eligibility or operational latency claim.

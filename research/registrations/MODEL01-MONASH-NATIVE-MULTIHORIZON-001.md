The full model starts from randomly initialized weights and learns from the authentic Monash customers. The source SHA and salted SHA client-role assignments are immutable: **176 FIT client identities**, **34 CAL**, **36 DEV**, **75 SEALED_TEST**. ## Frozen integrated native architecture and training

**Native model** retains original 103,252p TAC-OSM fast32, slow32, associative 8×8 gated-delta state, parallel token encoder+FFN, and a new **two-output 1h/24h forecast head**. Actual trainable and deployed params must be **103,381** (103,252+129), with no dummy/frozen makeup params. Two heads share full model and recurrence; no separate independently retrained heads. This is a capability development study, **not a matched architecture superiority claim**. No new GRU training required to claim that native model can be trained.

**Fixed features:** 48 prior hourly measurements; log1p hourly kW, standardized using mean/std of only FIT clients; two deterministic source-index clock sin/cos features. Original outputs in standardized logkW, inverse expm1/nonnegative load for scoring. No future meter, original DEV mean, tenant identity label embedding or hidden target. Scale and clipping derive from FIT-only statistics / fixed numeric boundedness, not late DEV optimization.

**Source FIT examples:** seed-controlled uniform sampling of FIT original client identity and hourly anchor with at least 24 nonzero observations in previous 168 source hours, excluding original source-local Portugal DST +-2-day anomaly dates. Two factual labels from hours `t+1` and `t+24` known only during FIT training; never as model inputs. Exact batch sampler identity SHA256 and `torch.save` weights + AdamW optimizer + PyTorch/Python/NumPy RNG state preserved. Three fixed original experiment seeds **1801,1802,1803**, **160 updates ×64 training examples**, AdamW lr=.001, weight decay=.01, smoothL1 loss beta=.5, global gradient clip1, no early stopping. Three exploratory training seeds are not final independent qualification (5 seed preferred when assessing scientific robustness).

**CAL:** 34 distinct customer IDs, evaluate exactly same 336 fixed target anchors per client as original baseline with the identical history/activity/DST gating; calibrate one factual global 90%-nominal absolute forecast residual quantile per horizon with no DEV labels. *Temporal/customer correlation invalidates iid conformal guarantee*. **DEV:** 36 distinct customer IDs, exact original no-fit baseline eligible anchors and target identifiers; score every seed, every physical customer, horizon 1h/24h. Report **per-client MAE and RMSE kW**, mean over clients as primary and overall decision count, global interval score/nominal90 empirical coverage/width secondary. Save all per-client outcomes keyed by SHA256-truncated publisher ID; no raw publisher series committed.

## Before training, fixed evaluation/falsification

Original best practical no-fit source references **on 36 DEV clients** are at h1: last observed 146.8676 kW client-macro MAE; at h24: same hour prior week 191.5431 kW, each **12,096** original DEV prediction decisions. Same original-source CAL no-fit references, features and source observability warnings remain source-owned. No use of DEV scores to change training recipe, seeds, horizons, thresholds or abort expensive seeds.

**Minimum evidence for a promising capability:** all original three seeds must record MAE for both horizons; three-seed mean client-macro MAE must be at least **5% below the best no-fit reference at *both* horizons** and avoid clear interval/coverage deterioration. Any shortfall => `DEV_MODEL_FAILS_PRACTICAL_BASELINE` or `DEV_MIXED_TRADEOFF`. Even a strong DEV win is **not independently confirmed** and cannot support investor/production/generalization claims before a separately preregistered, truly untouched source-heldout evaluation with client-cluster paired uncertainty. If model predicts negative/infinite, source or numerical checks fail and no metrics are promoted.

## Original hypothesis
[LANGPROFILE060](https://github.com/FPC-effortless/TAC-OSM/pull/599) found original `_advance` source lines 117/118 among the largest instrumented inclusive line costs; [LANGKERNEL061](https://github.com/FPC-effortless/TAC-OSM/pull/600) measured one paired ordinal-code vectorization about 3× faster at the isolated mathematical kernel, bitwise identical to original for 8195 clock positions each in FP32/FP64. This **does not guarantee** full decoder speed.

LANGDECODE062 copies original pinned `_advance` into a separate subclass and substitutes only the two positional ordinal encodings with one paired vectorized operation. Original 499623-parameter seed1701/FIT320 checkpoint unchanged, same original trained model, original reference validation, recurrent state, source tokens, masks, cumulative context, FFN and snapshot serialization.

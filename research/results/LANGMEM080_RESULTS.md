This is **not generalizable statistical efficacy**, independent data, memory fact reconstruction or a beneficial language architecture gain. The original 079 full carried DEV NLL of new dual was **worse** than 070 fast-only on all three seed pairs and remains the governing predictive quality result.

## Interpretation and next falsifiable experiment

LANGMEM077 identified source-native fast contraction α~0.5–0.6. LANGMEM079 introduced source-native slow α~0.990 that actually retains82.7% of one pre128 perturbation at16 bytes, but no next-byte quality gain. LANGMEM080 confirms even at the level of **one real historical latent source write**, the slow lane retains matrix content through large delays and the current query sometimes partially aligns, **yet its effect on output logits is tiny and can be harmful**.

This supports investigating at least three distinct causes instead of increasing rho further: **(i)** low learned slow write magnitude and read mixing; **(ii)** historical address collisions and weak source-conditioned query-to-writer selectivity (geometry alone not enough); **(iii)** learned decoding to byte logits from the old slow value, absent any explicit delayed-source reconstruction supervision. The next study should register original source event identity and honest delayed source content/byte retrieval labels from *original observed past only*, teacher-visible query context without future target labels, matched wrong-key/oracle controls, and new independently frozen licensed unseen publisher DEV before any promotion. Training-time slow token memory must be tested on true longer carried histories, not only reset128 original 078 windows. Never claim new model superiority vs SSM/Transformer without matched training FLOPs/data/parameters.

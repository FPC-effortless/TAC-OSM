# DEV063 belief integrity — implementation prototype, NOT a trained result

The new active goal authorizes a fresh mechanism question. Prior R1 fitting and
failed competitive claims stay closed, not reopened as another lucky recipe.
No new belief checkpoint is trained or promoted by this design file.

A posterior-only controller needs enough information to act: the old four health
states alone omit the public dependency graph and would handicap planning.
The prototype therefore exposes a supplied12-state joint vocabulary: four health
states x three relation structures (none,0->1,1->0). Both factors are predicted by
learned weights. Policy/future/cost heads read this semantic distribution plus
current public choices/identity binding/prices; they have no direct history latent.
This is an explicit schema prior, not discovered symbols or posterior-free learning.
No relationship table or analytical physical update is copied into inference.

Two temporal encodings are mechanically tested: gaps in admitted event counts
(for this time-independent synthetic world) and relative public time with explicit
unit metadata. First observations have unknown prior time, encoded zero; history
is bounded64. Event gaps deliberately discard physical durations; do not apply
that prior to real time-dependent machines. Relative physical intervals change
when actual duration changes. Origin shifts and corresponding unit conversions
must preserve outputs, not arbitrary physical speedups.

Property checks hold the12-state belief fixed while changing the history latent:
action/future/cost outputs remain identical. Joint probabilities still have a
nonzero policy learning path. These are structural tests, not evidence for trained
belief use, safe stopping, CASM superiority or real-world capability.

Next registration must freeze a20-seed factorial of bypass vs bottleneck and
absolute vs relative encoding; joint-belief capacity/information must match controls.
It must add fresh H32/128/512/1024 worlds, public-posterior exact/shuffle/blur
interventions, decision-relevant action flips, per-decision p95/p99 KL, all-stratum
physical faults, cost, learning curves, CPU and no-CASM/GRU controls. Near-zero
exact-posterior flips can mean accuracy, not decoration; shuffled/blurred beliefs
must change decision-relevant evidence to diagnose sensitivity. Freeze thresholds,
fitting budget and untouched evaluation before any new training.

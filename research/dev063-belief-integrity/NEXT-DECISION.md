The next mechanism question is semantic-interface stability. The restricted
actor uses its belief vector, yet exact-posterior replacement changes 35.3% of
actions, both tail gates fail, and higher sensor noise leaves physical faults.
Joint decision gradients may encourage a private encoder/actor code rather than
a calibrated public-state distribution. The curves support investigating this;
they do not establish the cause. Action changes can also be equivalent under
the objective, so evaluate outcome regret rather than optimizing flip rate alone.

A fresh, source-frozen comparison should isolate policy gradients through belief
from actor exposure to training-only semantic targets. Compare coupled versus
gradient-isolated/frozen belief learning, and predicted-only versus mixed
predicted/exact public posterior inputs during actor training. Hold data, proper
belief losses, update counts, initializations and validation search budgets fixed.
Include retrained GRU and no-CASM controls. Use at least 20 fresh training seeds,
new heldout worlds, the unchanged primary calibration thresholds and an explicit
higher-noise reliability guard. Measure closed-loop consequences of exact versus
predicted beliefs; no oracle or stop override may enter deployment.

Real forecasting is an independent failed gate. A future baseline study needs
a committed new design and genuinely unused evaluation before fitting; July B
remains unopened here. Domain precision tolerances, forecast horizon and practical
headroom must be set from training/public sensor specifications, never from a
favorable test channel. Do not promote a persistence fallback as a learned gain.

The acquired LTPP records provide actual maintenance costs and measured condition,
but not a complete factory task contract or inspection economics. Audit time gaps,
cointerventions, agency cost definitions, quality flags and out-of-site support
before any registered action-outcome model. Missing costs remain unknown. Neither
this source nor synthetic success supports a commercial viability claim yet.

CASM's named chained-task deadline and posterior-free learning remain separate
goals. More layers, SSMs or sparse operators do not resolve the present calibration
and real-data failures by themselves. Promote only a model that passes its gates;
choose a simpler control if additional mechanisms do not earn their cost.

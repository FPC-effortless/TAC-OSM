# Original public measurement archive

Chen, S. (2017). Beijing Multi-Site Air Quality [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5RK5G . Licensed CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . The air data originate from Beijing Municipal Environmental Monitoring Center; meteorology from China Meteorological Administration, matched by the publisher to nearest weather stations. This is the original nested archive, unchanged, extracted from the checksum-recorded official UCI download; no interpolated or fabricated measurements. See ../../mathematical-engineering/me100/SOURCE.json. Source-missing NA values are preserved. No cause/latency/health-intervention labels are supplied. Archive contains later dates for integrity; the experiment's parser rejects numerical rows from2016onward.

## Concrete objective and fixed design

The native ES-WAM (90D fast, 90D slow, finite 8-address × 3 evidence-role exchange memory) learns actual next physical 14D state from the eight available past original frames and previously recorded issued qpos. It is augmented by (1) causal measured velocity x[t]-x[t-1], (2) an auxiliary probability of real next-frame movement, (3) rational motion-probability-dependent suppression of unsupported small displacements, and (4) a bounded next-logged-command residual anchored to the previous observed issued command. The earlier unconditional absolute tanh action head was catastrophically worse than repeating the source command. The new head predicts a historical command, not an authorized physical policy.

For each next-state forecast, d is the native state residual plus an observed history-only clipped velocity term with learned bounded gain. The learned p_move is used to form tau=softplus(s)*(1-p)/(p+0.05), and the final relative-state residual is d*d^2/(d^2+tau^2+1e-12). This is selective forecasting, not a guaranteed skill or calibrated multi-joint probability. The movement target is defined only from factual future x[t+1] after prediction, at 0.025 mean absolute physical-unit displacement. No future authority labels, actual future motion flags, task success, action outcome or counterfactual result enter model inputs.

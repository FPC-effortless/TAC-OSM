# ME101 idea execution ledger

The owner requested capability-first development and faster testing, with cheaper
coordinated agents and attribution deferred. This cycle uses two bounded delegated
tasks: mathematical formulation/source verification and an advisory idea queue.
The owner reviewed both, reconciled the source identity, ran the complete local
suite and pinned the original mathematical source before any training.

Only ME101 is a newly authorized neural fitting study here. Other queue entries
are proposals, not hidden trials. ME101 has one fixed five-seed, fresh-initialized
run,3,072 updates/model, batch16, mixed8/64/256history, no extra tuning, maximum two
one-thread CPU fit workers. All five seeds stay in the results regardless of gate
outcome. The actual source/preprocessing and complete sampled histories are saved
with original models; CPU/steps/exposures are measured, not matched by claim.

No four-way source/matrix ablation or new GRU/Transformer fitting is required for
this integrated capability-development run. Architectural-superiority claims
would require a different fully matched registration. The same exposed2015cohort
is used for descriptive before/after development;2016–2017 numerical outcomes
remain closed. This run must not be called independent qualification.

The raw interval-width audit is supplementary reporting over saved forecasts.
It does not alter a forecast, calibrate a threshold, add an optimizer step or
change the registered acceptance gate. The original ME100 failed full gate is
preserved as the motivation, not reclassified into success.

Prior mathematical foundations: affine Gaussian marginalization and total
variance, heteroscedastic probabilistic forecasting and proper interval scoring
are established methods. The contribution being tested is their integrated
implementation with native TAC source traces and associative memory, not the
invention of these methods. No automatic calibration guarantee is assumed.

## Capability hypothesis

Can the new complete native 100,343-parameter exchange-structured fast/slow world-action model provide a useful factual one-step next-physical-state forecast on the original registered BodenAI robot observational stream? Use true recorded previous issued qpos, not a counterfactual current control choice. An additional head estimates the subsequent *recorded* action as behavioral imitation, not safer/better action choice. Observe gains/negative evidence against frozen ME076 and persistence/servo on the exact same real queries. Do not infer distinct-entity memory, long-delayed recall, general cross-site physical action, verified outcomes or real causal effects.

## Frozen baseline models and policy

- `P0`: global empirical next-byte frequency smoothed by Dirichlet 1/2 per each of 256 output bytes: `p0(y)=(count(y)+0.5)/(N+128)`.

For each k=1,2,3 historical source bytes (not a 3-gram over total token width), use the original training FIT sequence counts `c_k(h,y)` and `N_k(h)`; calculate `p_k(y|h)=(c_k(h,y)+8 p_(k-1)(y|suffix(h)))/(N_k(h)+8)`. If context history <k, or N_k(h)=0, inherit the lower-order distribution unchanged. This guarantees normalized positive laws; no hyperparameter tuning.

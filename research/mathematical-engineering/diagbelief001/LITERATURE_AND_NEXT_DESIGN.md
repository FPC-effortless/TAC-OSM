# Primary research review and TAC-specific implications

Primary abstracts were retrieved for10papers (PRIMARY_LITERATURE.json). Full texts of CQR, ACI, PCGrad and NeuralCDE were downloaded and relevant methods/theorem assumptions inspected; hashes inFULL_TEXT_RECEIPTS.json. Others below are abstract-level review plus established mathematical distinctions, not replication of their experiments. No claim of having verified every published proof. The current audit adds no model variant or training.

| Problem | Primary source and useful mechanism | Important limit; application to native TAC |
|---|---|---|
| Missing observations | [GRU-D, Che et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01865): masks and elapsed-time decay expose informative missingness. [Neural CDE, Kidger et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.08926): controlled dynamics incorporate new observations. | ODE evolution alone does not add observations or guarantee uncertainty. NeuralCDE Section3 uses a natural cubic spline, which can depend on subsequent knots; any online adoption requires a causal interpolation boundary. Retain TAC's masks/ages/source admission and input-conditioned affine scan; borrow evidence-aware innovation supervision rather than replacing it with an ODE/SSM. |
| Imputation | [BRITS, Cao et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10572): joint recurrent imputation and observed-data consistency. | A backward pass within the already observed prefix is legal offline; one using observations after the prediction cutoff leaks future information. Online forward reconstruction is the appropriate candidate here. It must actually update the reconstruction mean, currently blocked by typed routing. |
| Probabilistic forecasts | [DeepAR, Salinas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.04110): shared probabilistic recurrent prediction trained across related series. | A likelihood head does not by itself separate epistemic and aleatoric uncertainty or guarantee gap calibration. Use coherent current-belief/future-emission distributions and proper scores. Distribution support, censoring and transformation Jacobian must agree. |
| Uncertainty | [Deep ensembles, Lakshminarayanan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.01474): multiple fitted predictors capture some model disagreement. | Total mixture variance is mean(component variance)+variance(component mean), not mean of interval endpoints. Five seeds are not five independent datasets. Ensemble deployment adds weights/latency and must satisfy the budget if made a candidate; this audit does not ensemble/rescore REPORT. |
| Sharp coverage | [CQR, Romano et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03222), Section2/coverage theorem: held-out calibration of conditional quantiles. | Guarantee is finite-sample **marginal** coverage under exchangeability, generally at least nominal, not exact conditional coverage. Quantile loss/log-space alone guarantees neither interval narrowing nor a fixed50–80%gain. Current repeatedly exposed chronological cohorts are not fresh calibration evidence. |
| Drift | [ACI, Gibbs & Candès](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.00170), Section4.1/Proposition4.1: update miscoverage level after outcomes arrive. | Long-run empirical coverage differs from per-time/channel/gap conditional coverage. The proof allows infinite prediction sets outside quantile range. A bounded-width product cannot silently inherit that theorem. Every outcome must arrive before it updates a future forecast; original frozen receipts remain immutable. |
| Competing objectives | [PCGrad, Yu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06782), Section4/Theorem1; [MGDA multitask, Sener & Koltun](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04650). | PCGrad's cited theorem assumes two convex differentiable tasks and a Lipschitz gradient/controlled step; it can stop at opposing gradients. Neither method guarantees every finite AdamW step improves every heldout metric. Actual retained U-gradients here mostly align. Repair output constraints/semantic routing before generic projection. |
| Associative retrieval and parallel math | [DeltaNet parallelization, Yang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.06484): structured delta updates and efficient sequence computation. | Use operator conditioning, normalized addressing and separate write/read validation as mathematical inspiration; no Transformer/SSM conversion. Unit normalization does not force query-key alignment. Fixed-size compressed memory cannot losslessly retain arbitrary history. Current Temporal audit does not establish associative-memory superiority or diagnose all six families. |

## Corrections to the broader proposed remedies

- Sparse experts still share routers, encoders and data; expertise does not make interference impossible. Add specialized residual FFNs only with explicit gradient/data contracts and counted parameters. ME109/110 already have role FFNs; adding more without fixing their semantics is not justified by this audit.
- PSD+PSD is PSD, not necessarily strictly positive-definite. State covariance, future noise covariance and mean dependence must share a well-defined conditional model; a decoder Jacobian evaluated at one mean is a local approximation unless the emission is explicitly affine with stated conditioning. Cross-terms cannot be omitted without assumptions.
- A fixed unconditional24hour difference extrapolated linearly with age is not a statistical lower bound on conditional variance. Stationary/mean-reverting processes give direct counterexamples. Numerical positivity and statistical conservatism are different requirements.
- RAG/vector stores are external growing memory, not free expressivity within608bytes. A language model's FFNs and attention are distributed learned computations, not guaranteed isolated “facts” and “reasoning” modules. Language/counterfactual competence requires relevant training and evaluation; numerical forecasting alone does not imply it.
- Physical residual baselines are appropriate only when observation/action timing and units match. A physics-informed penalty does not make wrong servo dynamics correct. Logged-action prediction does not identify interventions without support/confounding assumptions; Dreamer-style imagined control also needs real environment qualification.
- Freezing base weights/adapters preserves original bytes, not general adaptation performance. No theorem guarantees MAML adaptation in3–5samples or SSM retention over arbitrarily long histories.

## Bounded next candidate, not another collection of mechanisms

1. Preserve ME110's improved point predictor, native fast/slow/8x8affine memory, streaming interfaces and original results. Start a new registration if any learned graph/update changes; no more ME110fits.
2. Define a typed conditional current-state distribution and future innovation distribution. Specify precisely whether current mean is a reconstructed physical latent state or merely a learned future residual. Allow real hidden-current targets to update the appropriate mean coordinates with a controlled route. Current P-only point learning cannot provide that supervision by itself.
3. Separate a small explicit numerical scale floor from a learned conditional uncertainty process. Estimate missing-state error with causal masked observations; learn or identify staleness dynamics fromFIT only. Preserve the historical floor/results and register the replacement and validation gates before training. Do not assert monotone uncertainty under new informative cross-channel evidence; absence of all relevant new evidence is a different condition.
4. Align uncertainty and point training populations across channel/availability/age, and measure both tail misses and proper interval/NLL scores. Keep per-channel criteria: PM overcoverage cannot compensate for NO2 undercoverage. Keep unchanged existing gate outcomes in historical tables.
5. Retain a fixed bounded five-seed exposure schedule and saved learning curves. More epochs alone are already contradicted as a joint remedy by late ME110FIT interval deterioration. Before fitting: source/transform/cache boundaries, live95k–105kcounts, no dummy parameters, Python forward/backward references, PNDS Lean proof assumptions and reciprocal record, original source/plan freeze.
6. After bounded exposed-FIT development, qualify once under a separately preregistered untouched cohort protocol. No retrospective scores or CI/proofs can substitute for that step. No auto-merge, autonomous control or commercial safety claim.

# ME110 read-only leakage audit

Scope: frozen source inspected in `/tmp/tac-me110`; AGENTS and local fpc-universal skill read, anti-cheat protocol consulted. No original source archive, trained checkpoint, REPORT tensors or sealed numerical data read; no training or source edits. This is source/fixture evidence, not full pipeline certification.

## Findings

**No confirmed forbidden REPORT/TEST-to-FIT or direct target-to-forward leakage found in inspected code.** ME100 loader permits only FIT/REPORT (scripts/me100_sensor_data.py:15–18), stops on year before numerical conversion (25–30), and uses only `transformed[:,:FIT_STOP]` for means/std (44–47). FIT is before 2015, REPORT before 2016 (12–13). Loader hashes the entire archive (17), so “no later numerical parsing” is accurate while “no later bytes read” would not be. Reading compressed archive bytes for SHA is not feature leakage.

Actual model invocation consumes only x[:6] in training (scripts/me110_typed_training.py:224–228) and scoring (348–350). ME100 batch histories end at origin and targets are +6 (me100_sensor_data.py:75–80). Augmentation copies history, zeros hidden numeric values, and recomputes hidden ages from strictly earlier observed records (me102_gap_data.py:74–79,111–126). Current reconstruction truth and future labels are separate returned tensors (81–85,128–133); no evidence they enter forward inputs.

**Confirmed retrospective exposure, correctly disclosed, not an independent holdout.** FAIR_COMPARISON.json:46 explicitly says ancestors/normalizers saw FIT and overlapping histories remain. Sampling excludes only identical monitor origins from current update origins, not their event windows (me110_sampling.py:38–43). Means/std and floor_rates use the entire FIT period (me100_sensor_data.py:45–47; me102_gap_data.py:36–45), so early FIT query transforms incorporate later FIT observations, including potential monitor labels. This is permitted full-training-partition fitting under the registered fixed-transform evaluation boundary; it would fail a claim of strictly online transform fitting or fresh FIT holdout. REPORT is the previously exposed 2015 cohort, explicitly disclosed (FAIR_COMPARISON.json:63); repeated historical development prevents independent qualification regardless of absence of mechanical leakage.

**Coverage gap: poison starts after fitted transforms, so it cannot clear raw-source-to-feature isolation.** me110_tools.py:36–39 changes one raw PM2.5 target at one origin but holds precomputed values/observed/ages/mean/std/floor rates fixed; output explicitly says normalization_cache_fixed_during_poison=True (60). It tests the late raw-target extraction boundary and fixed-checkpoint forward path, not parsing, normalizer fitting, missingness/selection, other future channels or source joins. OPEN entries (14–15,52) correctly retain full CSV partition and transform-fit provenance as INCONCLUSIVE. A poisoned FIT target would legitimately alter global FIT mean/std if rebuilt, whereas a REPORT-only poison must not alter FIT transforms.

**Coverage gap: phase tags do not authenticate array provenance.** floor_rates validates shape and phase but includes every time in raw (me102_gap_data.py:24–45). augmentation uses ntime rather than canonical FIT_STOP (69–73). Actual caller fit_data() returns data.load('fit') (me109_residual_training.py:74–80), and ME110 sampler independently bounds origins to [255,FIT_STOP-6) and station IDs (me110_sampling.py:8–16). Thus no actual contaminating path established; forged/mislabeled arrays could bypass helper-only assertions. End-to-end source receipt remains necessary.

**Target-dependent availability is explicit cohort conditioning.** General and gap origins require PM2.5 observed at +6 (me100_sensor_data.py:64–65), while gap selection uses causal PM age (70). This is complete-case evaluation on observable targets, not numeric answer leakage into forward; cannot establish performance on all future queries or missing-target processes. Baselines and model share frozen origin arrays (me110_typed_training.py:330–335).

**Score boundary is favorable by inspection but source audit is incomplete.** score calls budget_audit first, initializes from FIT and loads final model (322–343), and no optimizer is created in score. Rawscale is FIT-only (338). Predictions use newly initialized state per batch (349). Truth/masks for primary metrics come from historical ME101 arrays while NLL uses loader targets (350–352): origins are checked, but truth/mask identity against freshly loaded target arrays is not checked here. No demonstrated mismatch; feasible missing consistency assertion.

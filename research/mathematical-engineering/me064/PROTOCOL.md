# ME-064 prospective task-level real-robot experiment — before seeing held-out outcomes

This is a registered PROSPECTIVE multi-task, **one-task-out** experiment on original publisher robotic observations, not a claim of completed training or statistically verified superiority.

**Frozen approach:** 5 task names previously analyzed in development, 32 authentic whole FIT episodes per task + 8 whole CAL episodes per task (200 original training/calibration episodes total). All whole 4-episode recording blocks are hash-assigned from metadata only. `clean_stains_off_tabletop` selection is restricted to original pre-existing FIT membership to avoid its previously exposed original TEST. Train 2 architecture arms TAC native fast/slow/associative and equal-budget trained GRU on a single mixed-task source, with 600 updates per seed, batch 8, 5 new paired seeds, identical sampled task/window schedule. The single entirely unvisited target task is chosen via task-name hash from `install_spray_nozzle_onto_bottle`, `plug_charger_into_power_strip`, `replace_tissue_box_and_pull_out_first_tissue` before ANY target outcome is opened. This is an actual one-task-out train/test separation, **not** full multi-fold cross-validation or independent site/robot evaluation. Remaining unvisited candidates are left untouched for subsequent confirmation.

**Assumptions:** All registered tasks must have original LeRobot 14D `observations.state.qpos` and `action.qpos`, 15 FPS r1_lite, authentic `intervention` and `control_source`, complete strictly ordered time. Full source and pinned HuggingFace revision SHA and each original parquet episode SHA tracked in a Git-owned (data-license respecting) manifest. FIT-only mean/std and servo observer identified over all five task FIT cohorts together; CAL and target must never contribute to fitting them. Avoid mixing adjacent episodes across FIT/CAL or across domain tasks and identify episodes by compound (task, episode) keys.

**Primary hypothesis:** Multi-task real training makes learned physical state/servo/neural regime selection portable to a genuinely unseen robotic manipulation task. Test fixed TAC and fixed trained GRU checkpoints against heldout per-episode MAE, last-state persistence, FIT identified servo, and their own source-only calibration. TAC must be >=2% below **each** of GRU, persistence and servo with original recording-block clustered paired 95% upper interval below zero. Nonzero error advantage alone not enough. Secondary source development CAL and task-specific residual/physical-gain diagnostics; never score an unselected holdout or choose after results.

**Fair training** before fits: measurable trainable parameter gap <1%, identical 600 x batch8 original task/window picks in each paired seed, identical history64+horizon16, single multi-task FIT identification, same signed innovation/authority masks, objectives and learning schedule. Supported-operator training FLOPs profiled with PyTorch; <1% difference compulsory. Report unsupported/optimizer FLOPs unknown, CPU as an efficiency measure NOT compulsory equal. The prefit FIT-only receipt MUST be committed to repository before frozen full compare manifest and any optimizer update.

**Mandatory retained replay evidence:** Source source-hashes/FIT/CAL/target episode hashes and publisher license; 10 original model+optimizer+RNG trained checkpoint bytes with exact SHA-256, per-seed per-task sample order hash, code + physics identification, original nominal optimization trace, and end-to-end independent clean checkout model restoration. Checkpoint bytes must be Git-owned at `research/replay-bundles/ME064/checkpoints/` and available to reproduce any result; Actions artifacts and local-only tarballs do not substitute. Independently rerun real source and heldout scoring from pinned public legal source before claiming numerical audit complete.

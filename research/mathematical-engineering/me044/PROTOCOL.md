## Architectural objective

Build a stronger complete TAC model while preserving its strong context predictor during training. The new path gives native context-seeded fast32/slow32/8x8 error-corrected memory a separate horizon decoder. Six causally paired past patches feed native dynamics. Decode16 coefficients in an orthonormal multiscale forecast basis: eight piecewise-linear hats plus sin/cos harmonics1,2,4,8 of known daily sampling period (144 Tetouan ten-minute samples,24 ETTh1 hourly samples). QR basis construction in float64, canonical diagonal signs, RMS-normalized float32 buffer stored in every checkpoint. All matched controls receive the same cadence priors and basis. No calendar labels, future measurements or extra input channels.

Keep full original width50 context encoder, linear AR, horizon head and their initialized coefficients. Native decoder sees its state plus detached contextual latent/statistics. A separate learned trust gate bounds its correction to half the local window scale; no inherited forced decay to zero at720. Observed-future projected residual coefficients provide an auxiliary supervised target, not inference-time access. This combines established mathematical ideas; global novelty is not claimed.

Matched alternatives use an equally sized stateless192->37->64 context branch or six-token GRU40+projection64 instead of native memory, with identical separate basis decoder/trust/readout/training.

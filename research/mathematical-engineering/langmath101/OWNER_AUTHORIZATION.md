LANGMATH101 is the bounded trained companion to LANGMATH100. The owner requested: “Proceed and complete all the research stages.” The specific comparison instruction is: “Compare A0 against A3 at nominal depths of 1, 2, 4 and 8 blocks, first under matched parameters and then under matched training FLOPs.”

That instruction defines two separate experimental lanes. The parameter/exposure lane fixes 512 updates and the original mixed-source schedule. The counted-arithmetic lane consumes an unedited prefix of the same frozen schedule until the actual instrumented dense arithmetic target is first reached. Actual exposure differences remain outcomes. Neither lane is labelled a simultaneous fully matched architecture comparison. The ordinary generic strict comparison gate remains unchanged; an exact study-specific dispatcher validates this owner contract.

The current prospective model-size rule is retained: 95,000–105,000 active learned parameters, with at most one percent pairwise gap. The original three trained 10M LANGTRAIN099 checkpoints remain frozen historical evidence; these new fits do not silently recreate or scale those models.

All work is internal public FIT/DEV research under the existing internal-research amendment. There is no protected TEST access, independent certification or checkpoint promotion. Stage 009 requires an actual external assessor; a collaborating agent cannot supply independence. Stage 007 requires genuine withheld transformation-family data to establish the full requested generalization claim. These limitations must be reported even if all executable pilots complete.

Prior art includes causal attention (Vaswani et al., 2017, arXiv:1706.03762), linear attention recurrence (Katharopoulos et al., 2020, PMLR 119), byte latent patches (Pagnoni et al., 2024, arXiv:2412.09871) and dynamic hierarchical chunking (H-Net, arXiv:2507.07955). This pilot makes no novelty claim for stacking, multi-timescale state or patch representations.

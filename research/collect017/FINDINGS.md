# COLLECT017 — attention also fails under the fixed-budget copy recipe

This is an additional post-COLLECT016-null learnability control, not a change to
the original nine-fit primary comparison. Three 99,875-active-parameter models
completed all 512 updates on the exact C16 byte/target schedule for their seeds.
Native attention covers the full registered prefix with window1536 and retains
native fast/slow/rotary/SSA memories. It is not a pure Transformer or an unlimited
global-attention benchmark.

Every seed has 0% full-vocabulary first-byte, exact-four-byte, complete-map and
absent-first-byte accuracy in the registered primary. Restricted first-byte
accuracy is 12.5%, the balanced constant-class baseline. Local one/four-key,
familiar-key, recent TRAIN and post-null position-matched cases also fail.
Ordinary DEV NLL is 3.51177946, 3.48493408 and 3.49870805, mean 3.49847386,
seed SD 0.01342422. No seed/checkpoint selection or extra fitting was used.

The combined 12 models verify equal paired exposures and compatible initial
tensors/FFN slices. All source/checkpoint/journal/optimizer audits, trained
FP32/FP64 source references, causality/owner checks and finite 512-byte generation
checks pass. This is finite internal engineering verification, not independent
qualification or full Lean/IEEE/AD proof. The attention generation tensor payload
grows from 695,424 to 1,219,712 bytes at 256/512 generated bytes, within its window;
it must not be described as the phase lane's constant tensor payload.

The current bottleneck is establishing local binding, query/answer-mode learning
and usable optimization before interpreting retention/capacity. Full BPTT supplies
credit but did not teach the task; the native attention result prevents assigning
the null uniquely to matrix compression. Ordinary neural DEV losses also exceed
post-null TRAIN-only add-one unigram diagnostic losses. Those are unmatched
classical controls, not qualified architecture competitors.

No architecture advantage or global-attention replacement is demonstrated.
Bounded phase storage is a structural property shared with recurrent methods;
its utility must be shown at matched quality, state bytes and compute.
Typed summaries, biological memory, local contextual address composition,
decoupled admission and guarded exact archives are recorded as qualified
hypotheses, not implemented successes.

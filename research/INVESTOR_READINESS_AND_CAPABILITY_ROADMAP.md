## Investment thesis and honest current position

**Technical north star:** build TAC-OSM as an efficient, persistent, action-conditioned predictive world-state model that learns from authentic streams, retains and corrects useful state, forecasts future events and delays, and (only with verified real action/outcome support) estimates the consequences of different actions.

**Initial candidate product thesis, still to be validated:** an **advisory enterprise workflow intelligence engine** that ingests real, authorized timestamped events; tracks a case/entity's current belief; forecasts upcoming state/event sequences and waiting times; highlights risk of delay, exception or stalled progression; reports predictive uncertainty and relevant evidence; and supports an operator's decisions **without silently executing actions**. This is a wedge for testing value, not a permanent restriction on TAC's world-model research. Adjacent robotics/physical-world and cross-domain ambitions remain research lanes, not immediate proof of a shared commercial product.

## Two coequal objectives — make the model smarter and make its usefulness legible

Research priority is **capability FIRST**: integrate proven mathematics and train the *whole native model*. Commercialization requirements should **choose high-value real-world problems, access to lawful authentic data, and meaningful evaluation metrics**, not block training until sales arrive. A technical improvement is not automatically commercial value, and a successful sales pitch is not proof of model intelligence.

1. **World-state capability:** genuine entity continuity, inference-time state carry and verified correction from new observations; missing/irregular observations; long source-supported history; no fabricated missing events or future labels.
2. **Foresight:** next and multi-event/horizon prediction, online-known prefixes, real event/status transitions, delay/exception risk and case termination boundaries with actual observed-label eligibility; explicit abstention/coverage for unavailable outcomes.
3. **Probabilistic usefulness:** coherent event/no-event and time probabilities where appropriate; log loss, Brier/ECE, quantile pinball, proper interval scores, median/p90/p99 uncertainty widths, calibration drift; false-alert burden and advance warning lead time.
4. **Action-conditioned observed outcomes:** only train and evaluate where original timestamped records reveal *action available then*, outcome, cost and censoring. Observational correlations are not do-intervention causal effects. Decision and policy value must be measured separately under suitable evidence.
5. **Cross-domain learning:** later qualify a frozen complete model in an independently obtained real setting (e.g. second institution, different process, physical dynamics). Do not mix domains to inflate a single-application proof.

**No forced ablation loop:** a single native `capability_development` study may train and demonstrate new skills without additional attribution/control fits, provided it is transparent about DEV/exposed-test status. Only assert architectural superiority after appropriately resource-matched comparisons; the old 64-event rejection gate is for a *memory-superiority claim*, not a veto on development. Retain original in-repository checkpoints for every trained seed, legal source provenance and independent clean-clone score replay. CPU runtime is an efficiency metric, not a parity matching criterion.

## Initial wedge: enterprise workflow forecasting (candidate, not validated)

**Buyer/user hypothesis:** operations lead, service/process owner or risk/quality team with sufficiently costly delay/exception workflows and observable event logs. **Core interview question:** which already-measurable operational exception has enough cost, frequency and intervention lead time that a prediction would change a human decision?

**Narrow research-to-demo contract:**
- Input: authorized event stream with event-time/ingest-time distinction, stable verified entity IDs, observation availability timestamp, missingness and source version.
- Model output: current inferred state, next-event distribution, selected 1/2/4/8 future event horizons only where training/evidence support them, delay quantiles, uncertainty/abstention, evidence/provenance and update timestamp.
- Outcome: subsequently verified event/delay/exception; report no-outcome and censored cases separately. A source trace ending is not necessarily a verified real-world business completion.
- Advisory interface only: the model cannot directly approve, cancel, finance, service or otherwise act on clients. Human owner decides and final authority stays outside TAC.
- Pilot measurement: a version-frozen pre-period and prospective evaluation contract; reliable metric baselines and alert thresholds selected before pilot evaluation; domain-specific cost matrix signed off by the operator; measure net decision/operations improvement as well as prediction quality; record failures and drift.

**Product demonstrator milestone:** working versioned API and a minimal operator dashboard with current-state timeline, predicted future branches/times, changing beliefs and explicit uncertainty. Use anonymized/licensed demo inputs with clear provenance; synthetic fixtures are allowed for UI engineering **only** and must never be advertised as model performance.

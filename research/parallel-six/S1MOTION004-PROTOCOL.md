## Falsifiable root-cause question
S1MOTION003 found point/risk/uncertainty gradient conflict and poor signed correlation of the bounded velocity innovation to actual true residuals. These were observations, not causal explanations. **Hypothesis**: keeping auxiliary head learning but blocking ALL gradients from BCE and Gaussian NLL into the shared neural world-state backbone, and blocking the NLL point-loss term from the point decoder, improves *real* joint-state residual forecasting without harming robust motion regimes.

Keep exactly the shared six-family parallel TAC memory, all native weights, 14D x[t]/x[t-1], logged prior action u[t-1], affine scan and fixed velocity anchor/0.1 bounded innovation. **Same 95,907 trained weights and 166 FP32 carried scalars (664 bytes, excluding metadata/weights)**. No seventh family. No extra differentiable forward pass or new trainable parameters. Isolation mode computes original core point prediction with normal gradients and builds its **existing** 198->12->15 auxiliary observational head on *detached* fast/slow/associative-read/predicted features. For the auxiliary NLL, detach point forecast before calculating dispersion penalty. The risk/scale head still learns from actual FIT labels, while primary `MSE` alone reaches recurrent memory and point forecast. A native non-isolated TAC is trained with identical source and parameters to measure the intervention effect, plus equally constructed *same-velocity* GRUs with/without auxiliary detachment as controls. Pure deterministic velocity and persistence remain uncompromised.

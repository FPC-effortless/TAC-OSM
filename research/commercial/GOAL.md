# Goal: verified economical learned maintenance decisions

Deliver a portable trained agent that estimates current uncertainty from public
maintenance histories, predicts action consequences and selects inspect/repair/
STOP from learned weights. Success requires >=18/20 fresh training seeds attaining
>=95% completion on novel identities, no physical-reliability regression, calibrated
beliefs, competitive total verified-task cost against optimized statistical and
learned controls, strict independent-host replay, and an out-of-time real-data
maintenance evaluation with actual intervention costs and verified repair outcomes.
All components must earn promotion through registered equal-information controls.
This is a measurable goal, not a promise that research will achieve superiority.

## Work authorized and performed

The DEV061.5, DEV062A and conditional DEV062-R1 registrations retain complete
training distributions, validation pilots, failed gates, source and checkpoint
hashes. A real-data lane independently downloads and verifies MetroPT-3 and fits
teacher-free sensor predictors. Inference engineering preserves old weights and
rejects a slower cache; available-packet batching is qualified separately.
Original DEV058–061 artifacts remain unchanged. Draft integration only; no live
maintenance actions, paid services, merger or external messages are required.

## What qualifies as learned

Neural content updates, evidence reads, neural operator mixtures, predictions and
candidate/STOP scores are fitted. Public manifests, two-entity state/action schema,
hard identity binding, source-type gate, bounded FIFO and deterministic numerical
choice decoder are supplied structure. DEV061.5 additionally copies public
relationship keys; DEV062A/R1 remove that stream. Neither general entity discovery,
general symbolic CASM nor learned indexed acquisition is demonstrated. Exported
control contains no analytic repair policy, Bayes filter or completion override.
See ../../docs/LEARNED-BOUNDARY-DEV062.md for the complete boundary.

## Commercial evidence gate

MetroPT-3 is genuine maintenance-associated operational data, but not the requested
complete decision/outcome contract. Four reported fault intervals do not supply
available repair alternatives, inspection prices or independent post-repair success.
FMUCD supplies work-order costs but its inspected schema has no explicit verified
physical outcome field. No source is relabeled as causal repair evidence.
Research sensor prediction must beat persistence before product forecasting claims.
All sourced evidence, gaps, licensing and download status are in DATA-SOURCES.md.

The goal remains **not commercially qualified** until these external evidence and
performance gates pass. Missing outcomes cannot be repaired with synthetic labels,
a successful simulator replay, a neural completion score, or a longer research run.

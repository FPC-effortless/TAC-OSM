## E1: residual-triggered state-anchored repair

Per seed construct 16 random directed matchings of 32 eight-bit blocks using RNG seed+90000. Run the unchanged 004C targeted graph discovery once per world; verify against observations, no true hidden graph to repair. Introduce one dormant read on target action1 with a new parent outside original pair, present only at a local target state where its table1 and table2 differ, not the witness. Evaluate independent random snapshots and action1 on that target up to 2048 (not a natural trajectory; record right-censored no-discovery). Detect mismatch using a verified action and fully observed pre/post states. On the first mismatch, freeze cached execution and query a resettable simulator at that exact state: hold target and known parent fixed; clear bit0 of all unknown parents, then repeatedly retain only a half of the active unknown parent bits. Under the single-parent hypothesis, the effect is reproducible with a singleton; if not, abstain. Confirm candidate by complete local target sweep under isolated parent low/high, compare to known action1 and action2; infer a state-dependent gate mask. Revalidate at anchor and across 256 fresh random state/action cases per discovered world, half sampled at learned gate, half random; score errors before/after against simulator, count all interventions (including discovery and 512-row sweep), elapsed discovery/repair, bracket probes and detection delay. Record cached-plan invalidation boolean; this is a state-transition repair, not a complete replanner. No mathematical guarantee beyond single-parent bit0/local-gate family. Additional external AND gate challenge: two active unknown blocks required, no singleton suffices; test anchored repair rejects. No unconditional O(log B) claim; only localization under monotonic single-cause assumptions, not validation.
